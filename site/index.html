FusionSpace HPR

One suite for hobby and high-power rockets, written in Rust: a flight simulator first, then a flight analyzer, a competition kit and an app. Every product's source and releases live in one repository, nrdptel/fusionspace-eridanus, under the MIT or Apache-2.0 license.

HPR Sim is the one released so far. Before you rely on a number from it, read how far to trust a simulation: what it was checked against, and where it hasn't been.

Each product is numbered like a part on a drawing once it ships. Released means published and maintained; in preparation means not published yet.

Principles

The rules every FusionSpace tool is designed to. HPR Sim's docs give each model's equations, its sources and what it was validated against.

Show the working

Formulas, constants and sources are there to read, not hidden.

Say how far to trust it

Estimates are labeled as estimates, with their spread. Never a go/no-go verdict.

Work at the field

Readable in sun, with big targets for cold hands. HPR Sim's core needs no network.

No accounts, no tracking

This site sets no cookies and loads nothing from anyone else. It keeps one thing, your theme choice, in your own browser.
